Case Closing

Two steps in closing a case. Ready to close: every open or pending matter, checked for a filed Substitution of Attorney out (an SoA without "out" in the filename is us coming in, so it's ignored), Notice of Withdrawal, or Request for Dismissal (CIV-110) — a real signal a matter is done and was never closed. Only matters with a signed/conformed/conf document on our side are listed — an unsigned draft, a partial dismissal (TRO, RFO, joinder), or something found only in OP's own pleadings isn't shown. Close Case is never blocked on anything found here; this is a checklist, not a gate. A full scan takes several minutes, so it only runs when you click Run Audit. Trust to refund: closed matters with no unbilled work and no unpaid bills that still hold trust — process the refund in Clio.
